# Demo Vault

This vault ships with three connected notes so `exo index` and `exo search`
work out of the box. The shape to copy: typed frontmatter, plain note-ID
`relations:`, and body [[Wikilinks]] like this one to [[Hybrid Retrieval]].

Delete these notes and start writing your own. The [[Reading MOC]] shows what
a map-of-content note looks like.
